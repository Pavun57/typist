Listening…
More than dictation

A voice assistant that types, sends, remembers, and fixes your code.

⌨️

Voice commands

Say "send this" and your message is typed and sent. "Undo that", "new line", "select all" — keystrokes, not just words.

🧠

Memory

"Remember my address is X" — saved forever. Later, "type my address" drops it at your cursor. View and delete facts anytime.

👁️

Screen-aware coding help

"Solve this" screenshots the error and asks your local claude or codex CLI — no API key — then types the fix.

🧭

Context-aware typing

Paragraphs in email, one casual line in chat, literal text in your IDE. Typist knows which app you're dictating into and formats for it.

🗣️

99+ languages

Auto-detect, or pin English, Hindi, Tamil, Telugu, Bengali, Kannada, Malayalam, Marathi, Gujarati, Punjabi, Odia, and more.

🔐

Private by design

Go fully offline and nothing leaves your machine. API keys are encrypted with your OS keychain. MIT licensed, forever.

Your engine, your choice

Cloud speed or total privacy. Switch anytime.

Two speech-to-text engines, plus an optional AI cleanup pass that fixes grammar, enhances prompts, and detects commands — via free-tier Groq, OpenRouter, or NVIDIA keys.

Cloud

Sarvam AI

Fast and excellent for Indian languages.

  • saarika:v2.5 — built for Indic speech
  • Long dictations auto-chunked
  • Key stored encrypted in your OS keychain
Offline

Local models

Audio never leaves your device. One-time download, auto-unload when idle.

  • Whisper Base / Small / Large v3 Turbo — 99 languages
  • Dolphin CTC — fast, strong on Tamil, Hindi, Telugu
  • Omnilingual 300M — Meta's 1600-language model
How it works

Three keys to a sent message.

01

Press the hotkey

From any app, any input field. A small pill shows Typist is listening — click it to cancel.

02

Speak naturally

Dictate a message, a command, a fact to remember, or ask for help with what's on screen.

03

Press again — done

The transcript is polished and typed at your cursor. It's on your clipboard too, just in case.
